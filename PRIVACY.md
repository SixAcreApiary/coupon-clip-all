# Privacy Policy - Coupon Clip All

_Last updated: October 14, 2026_

Coupon Clip All is a browser extension that clicks the "Clip" buttons on the Kroger digital coupons page in your own browser.

## Data collection

The extension **does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.** It has no analytics, no tracking, no remote servers and makes no network requests of its own.

## Data stored on your device

The extension saves four settings using the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- your chosen delay between clips
- the on-page panel's position
- whether "Hide clipped coupons" is turned on
- whether "Hide Boost Exclusive coupons" is turned on

These never leave your browser. Removing the extension deletes them.

## Page access

The extension runs only on `https://www.kroger.com/savings/cl/coupons*`. It reads the coupon cards on that page to decide which "Clip" buttons to click. It does not read or store your account details, coupon history, or any other page content.

## Third parties

The panel includes an optional "Buy me a coffee" link to buymeacoffee.com. Nothing is sent there unless you click it, and that site's own privacy policy then applies.

## Affiliation

Coupon Clip All is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by The Kroger Co.
